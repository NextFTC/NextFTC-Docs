Before starting this tutorial, there are a couple things you'll want to have setup: NextFTC installed in your project, and your project organized in a way you want.

## Installing NextFTC

If you haven't added NextFTC to your project yet, head over to the [Installation](/introduction/installation/) page.
It walks through adding the dependencies to your `build.gradle` and getting everything available for you to use in your project.

## Structuring your project

NextFTC doesn't require any specific project layout, but it helps to have a consistent one.
The [Robot Project Structure](/robot/project-structure/#suggested-not-required-layout) shows an example of how you might organize your `NextRobot`, subsystems, and OpModes.
